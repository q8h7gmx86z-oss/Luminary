Luminary: a tour

Luminary is a personal cognitive training system. It builds a short daily workout from adaptive brain games, tracks a Brain Sharpness score, and explains the science behind every exercise. It is built for every kind of brain, including ADHD. This page is a static tour with real screenshots, so it works without JavaScript. The live app is at the home page of this site.

What Luminary does

  • Personalised daily workout. About 10 minutes, built from your last sessions: roughly 60% targets your weakest skill, 25% adds variety and 15% is familiar. Each pick shows the reason for it.
  • Six adaptive games across four skills: processing speed, working memory, attention control and mental flexibility. Every game levels up or down from your accuracy and speed.
  • Brain Sharpness score out of 1000, built from level and accuracy across the four skills, with a brain network that lights up as it rises. Progress is compared with your own baseline, never with other people. No leaderboards.
  • Daily challenge and weekly quest, with a forgiving streak: rest days count as part of training.
  • Focus track for ADHD: attention drills before focused work, plus a visual countdown timer.
  • Reasoning Assessment: 20 timed questions on patterns, numbers and words, with an experimental score. It is not an IQ test and is labelled that way.
  • The Rafi Programme: the 14 changeable dementia risk factors from the 2024 Lancet Commission, turned into a personal protection plan. Named for Rafaela "Rafi", 1947 to 2025.
  • Sleep and mood logs, with CBT-I sleep techniques and a quick mood, energy and focus check-in.
  • Learn: short, sourced explainers on why each exercise works.
  • Training preferences: goal, session length, challenge level, untimed mode, visuals, sound and breaks.
  • Private by design: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on the device and can be deleted in Settings. Under-18s get extra privacy protections.
